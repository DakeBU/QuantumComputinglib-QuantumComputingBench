# Supplementary natural-language mathematics workbook

The November 2026 intake joins two overlapping collaborator deliveries into one
cohort of twelve mathematical packets. The continuation archive contains the
original twelve unchanged packets and thirteen additional contribution files.
It does not provide a second set of questions.

The website organizes the material into three subject chapters and provisional
construction bands D2 (three), D3 (five) and D4 (four). D0 is a knowledge facet
of an exercise, not an extra question. Readers need no Lean installation.

## Identity and evaluation scope

Four packets provide additional expositions of existing SP006, LA015, SP007 and
SP114 contracts. Their original families, test/dev splits and mathematical
identities remain unchanged. The new exposition does not replace current gold.
Eight expanded targets remain candidates with unassigned splits. In particular,
the quantitative coherent-family phase problem is broader than an ordinary
same-contract view of LA016 or BE104; its sign example and phase foundations
already overlap existing material.

The workbook adds no canonical core, Lean admission or canonical train/evaluation
row. Its question-only JSONL, references, review index and node reverse index
are separate supplementary downloads. Shared normalization, rotation-distance
and phase ingredients must be reconciled before any future scored pooling.
Public problems and solutions are not a hidden holdout.

## Mathematics, sources and reader corrections

Fresh question-first derivations were saved before the three intake reviewers
opened the contributed answers. The exact answers, rubrics, source identities,
reasoning steps, AND inputs and boundary cases were then compared. These are
AI reviews, with exposure and limitations recorded; human expert approval and
new Lean execution are not claimed.

The raw contribution packages and their original adverse/revised review history
are preserved byte for byte. The intake record includes both archive hashes.
Separate reader addenda clarify equivalent grading routes and split conditional
boundary nodes without rewriting the originals. The precision route proves its
own strictness before invoking normalization stability. Given-only equation
companions have a separate source-bound notation comparison.

Every page links exact frozen graph anchors and source files, with current
exact-ID navigation distinguished from frozen-only identities. The reasoning
maps are authored mathematical routes, not extracted Lean proof dependencies.
The node-to-exercise map supports reverse inspection across this cohort.

Sealed intake review artifacts have an explicit `.gitattributes` exception that
preserves raw bytes and original line endings. Source/data files retain the
repository's LF policy. No line-ending normalization silently re-seals an
independent derivation or transfers an old approval.

## Reproduce the intake checks

```sh
python -m unittest website.scripts.test_qcb_nonlean_workbook -v
python benchmarks/quantumcomputingbench/scripts/curriculum.py validate
```

The tests check duplicate accounting, split boundaries, source/phase/notation
bindings, question/reference separation, exact download projections, original
question preservation and scope ancestry. Browser gates compile actual formulas,
open solutions/rubrics/maps by keyboard, check graph links and test mobile and
desktop layouts in all three website themes. Commit-pinned provenance links
must resolve to actual blobs in the publication commit before publishing.
