## Recommended integration disposition

Keep B2 **uncounted and unassigned** while the course owner decides whether its
general quantitative phase-family target deserves a separate core. It is usable
as an uncounted diagnostic extension with the shared phase foundations disclosed.
Do not present it as independent held-out evidence of those foundations. The
comparison supports a potential additional target; it does not establish global
novelty or compel admission.

If the owner instead groups it under an existing phase mechanism, preserve its
historical candidate UUID and review bindings and use the applicable explicit
diagnostic/view relationship. **Do not simply replace `core_id` by LA016 or BE104**:
the present B2 question is not the immutable nine-field contract of either core.
An ordinary same-core view must preserve that contract. An expanded diagnostic
must remain explicitly labelled and uncounted under its supported format; any
question-successor migration needs its own protocol and exact-content review.

No candidate payload, question, split, reservation or existing core was edited
by this checkpoint. The decision requested from a maintainer is concrete:
whether to admit the full quantitative-family target after global reconciliation,
or retain it as an uncounted diagnostic extension. Work on other source-backed
targets can continue while that decision is pending.
