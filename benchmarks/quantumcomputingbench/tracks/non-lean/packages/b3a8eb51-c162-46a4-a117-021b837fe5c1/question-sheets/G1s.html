Which adjacent gate could explain two population measurements?

Assumptions

  • The sites 0,…,N−10,\ldots,N-1 are arranged on a line, with N≥2N\ge2. The input is a real unit vector xx. Two exact population reports p,qp,q have nonnegative entries summing to one, with pj=xj2p_j=x_j^2. The input signs are unreported.
  • The proposed explanation is exactly one operation on an edge (i,i+1)(i,i+1). On that ordered pair it applies R(θ)=(cos(θ/2)−sin(θ/2)sin(θ/2)cos(θ/2))R(\theta)=\begin{pmatrix}\cos(\theta/2)&-\sin(\theta/2)\\ \sin(\theta/2)&\cos(\theta/2)\end{pmatrix} for an arbitrary real θ\theta, fixing every other coordinate.

Conventions

  • The angle may be zero. An explanation need only reproduce the output populations qjq_j; no output signs are specified.
  • Write Pk=∑j=0kpjP_k=\sum_{j=0}^{k}p_j and Qk=∑j=0kqjQ_k=\sum_{j=0}^{k}q_j for the mass to the left of the cut between kk and k+1k+1, where 0≤k<N−10\le k<N-1.

Question

Using the two lists of cut masses, find every edge on which one gate could have produced the second report. Give a necessary and sufficient test and justify it for every choice of signs consistent with pp, allowing the angle to depend on the actual input. Explain what the test says when the reports agree and when the input has zero mass on the proposed pair.

For N=4N=4, suppose p=(1,2,1,4)/8p=(1,2,1,4)/8. Diagnose each proposed output q(1)=(1,1,2,4)/8q^{(1)}=(1,1,2,4)/8 and q(2)=(2,2,1,3)/8q^{(2)}=(2,2,1,3)/8. For each, give all possible edge locations or explain why no single gate fits.
