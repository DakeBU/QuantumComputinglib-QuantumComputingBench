How many adjacent rotations prepare one signed state?

Assumptions

  • Let N≥1N\ge1. The real coordinate sites of a path are 0,…,N−10,\ldots,N-1. The known target x∈ℝNx\in\mathbb R^N has ∑jxj2=1\sum_jx_j^2=1. Its support is S={j:xj≠0}S=\{j:x_j\ne0\}. The input ese_s has amplitude +1+1 at the specified site ss.
  • A gate on edge (j,j+1)(j,j+1) applies R(θ)=(cos(θ/2)−sin(θ/2)sin(θ/2)cos(θ/2))R(\theta)=\begin{pmatrix}\cos(\theta/2)&-\sin(\theta/2)\\ \sin(\theta/2)&\cos(\theta/2)\end{pmatrix} to the two amplitudes in increasing site order and fixes all other amplitudes. Every real angle is allowed.

Conventions

  • One use of a gate costs one, regardless of angle. The empty circuit costs zero. There are no auxiliary sites, free sign changes or free permutations.
  • Preparation must output the particular signed vector xx. Only the action on ese_s is prescribed. Assign minimum cost +∞+\infty to an unreachable target.

Question

Find the exact minimum gate count for every xx and ss. Give a lower bound that applies to every circuit, including those that visit sites outside the target support. Provide a construction meeting the bound whenever the target is reachable. Account for holes in the support, both signs of a target supported only at ss, and the one-site path.
