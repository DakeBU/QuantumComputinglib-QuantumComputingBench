What can power traces recover about cycle phases?

Assumptions

  • Let XX be a finite labeled set of size N≥1N\geq1, and let pp be a known permutation. An unknown unitary acts on the labeled orthonormal basis by U|x⟩=ax|p(x)⟩U|x\rangle=a_x|p(x)\rangle, with |ax|=1|a_x|=1. For each oriented cycle CC of pp, let hC=∏x∈Caxh_C=\prod_{x\in C}a_x.
  • The data are exact complex numbers. Write tn=tr(Un)t_n=\operatorname{tr}(U^n) for the global power traces. One may use Newton’s identities, which recover a monic degree-mm polynomial from the first mm power sums of its roots counted with multiplicity.

Conventions

  • Cycles of length one are included. An arc follows the directed edge x↦p(x)x\mapsto p(x). A cycle product belongs to its labeled cycle unless the requested output explicitly asks for an unordered multiset. The characteristic polynomial is det(zI−U)\det(zI-U).

Question

Derive the characteristic polynomial and a formula for every positive power trace from the cycles and their edge weights. Suppose first that no two cycles have the same length. Using only t1,…,tNt_1,\ldots,t_N and the known permutation, recover each hCh_C on its own cycle and prove your procedure works even when one cycle length divides another.

Can all global power traces determine even the multiset of products for each length for an arbitrary known permutation? Decide this already for one permutation with two fixed points and two transpositions. If recovery fails, construct two unit-modulus weight assignments on that same permutation. Verify equality of all their power traces and a difference of product multisets within at least one length, so that relabeling cycles of equal length cannot remove the ambiguity.

Now augment the readout as follows. For each occurring length ℓ\ell, let mℓm_\ell count its cycles and let PℓP_\ell project onto the span of all basis vectors on those cycles. You receive tr(PℓUkℓ)\operatorname{tr}(P_\ell U^{k\ell}) for 1≤k≤mℓ1\leq k\leq m_\ell. Determine what product data these reports recover for each length. Prove recovery with multiplicities and explain precisely when these reports assign the recovered products uniquely to the labeled cycles.
