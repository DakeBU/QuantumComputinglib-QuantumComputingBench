Which phase references can be fixed on a permutation cycle?

Assumptions

  • Let XX be a finite nonempty set with a labeled orthonormal basis, and let pp be a known permutation of XX. Write 𝕋={z∈ℂ:|z|=1}\mathbb T=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|=1\}. Two devices act by Ua|x⟩=ax|p(x)⟩U_a|x\rangle=a_x|p(x)\rangle and Ub|x⟩=bx|p(x)⟩U_b|x\rangle=b_x|p(x)\rangle, with ax,bx∈𝕋a_x,b_x\in\mathbb T.
  • A diagonal calibration has D|x⟩=dx|x⟩D|x\rangle=d_x|x\rangle with dx∈𝕋d_x\in\mathbb T. A set S⊆XS\subseteq X of reference labels requires ds=ηsd_s=\eta_s for specified ηs∈𝕋\eta_s\in\mathbb T. The set SS may be empty.

Conventions

  • The calibration equation is the exact operator identity Ub=DUaD†U_b=DU_aD^\dagger. A cycle follows the arrows x↦p(x)x\mapsto p(x). Labels are fixed throughout, including labels in different cycles of the same length. A fixed point is a cycle of length one.

Question

Give a necessary and sufficient test, expressed in terms of the supplied edge weights and reference phases, for the calibration equation to have a solution. Construct every admissible DD and describe all its freedom. Your test should work for cycles with no reference, one reference or several, and for fixed points. Show which information survives when each edge phase around a cycle can be adjusted through a change of basis.

A technician has recorded only the product of the edge weights around each labeled cycle. Show that matching these records can suffice when there are no fixed references yet fail after two phases on one cycle are prescribed. Give two devices with the same permutation and matching cycle records, along with reference values that are incompatible. Identify where the obstruction is visible along the cycle.
