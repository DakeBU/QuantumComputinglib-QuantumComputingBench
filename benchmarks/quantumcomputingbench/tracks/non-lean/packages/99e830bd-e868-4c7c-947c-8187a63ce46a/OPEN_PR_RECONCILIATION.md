## Candidate overlap requiring a maintainer decision

**B2 versus the proposed Plan208 successor.** B2 assumes available branch
preparations and derives support-dependent phase alignment, an exact weighted
error formula and label-diagonal correction for an arbitrary finite family.
Plan208 asks for a phase-correct entry loader on the closed disk, unitary
completion, sparse block assembly and precision/resource boundaries, and also
assesses coherent index phases. The full targets differ as written, but the
coherent-phase subproof is shared. This is additional evidence for B2's already
high alias/specialization risk against LA016, BE104 and the legacy phase program.
No new-core count or split clearance is justified before reconciliation.

**A3 versus the proposed Plan208 successor.** A3 assumes an explicit induced-norm
approximate projected block and a positive overlap margin, then proves normalized
error, success bounds and fresh-trial repetition costs. Plan208 constructs a
conditional exact projected block from additional entry/sparse interfaces. They
share clean projection, scale and zero-output boundaries; A3's perturbative
conditioning target is not a repair or replacement of the reserved loader core.
Future integration must keep those shared obligations visible.
