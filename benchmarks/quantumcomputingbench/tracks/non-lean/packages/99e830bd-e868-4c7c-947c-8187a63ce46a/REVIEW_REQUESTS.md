# Remaining review requests

These are requests for the integration handoff. No reviewer invitation, message
or external approval was sent or fabricated.

1. **Semantic admission and split reconciliation.** A maintainer should compare
   each new candidate with the current native, reserved and legacy targets and
   the 16 accessible open PRs in `OPEN_PR_RECONCILIATION.md` and any later
   contributions. B2 has the strongest alias or
   specialization risk against existing phase questions. B3 uses the legacy
   circuit-error diagnostic as a supplied interface; its optimization and
   rounding target must be reviewed on that basis. A2/A3/C4 intentionally share
   a normalization lemma. Equivalent views and shared tested subproofs do not
   become independent held-out samples.
2. **Existing-core view integration.** A1, A4, B1 and C1 are proposed complete
   answer/rubric/exposition views under unchanged nine-field contracts. Decide
   how to integrate them through the existing view or answer-review workflow.
   They do not supersede current answers merely by appearing in `proposals/`.
   No question-successor migration is requested for those four cores.
3. **Mathematical/source accountability.** Inspect the independently derived
   solutions and complete content-bound comparison reports, including limitations
   and the corrected pre-freeze C2 finding. These are actual AI-agent reviews,
   not human approval, complete paper audits or new Lean execution.
4. **Grading usability.** Try the partial-credit rubrics on genuinely different
   correct derivations and plausible partial answers. No model inference or
   scoring calibration was performed here. Rubric arithmetic and schema checks
   cannot determine whether a criterion is pedagogically useful.
5. **Optional later formal alignment.** If maintainers want a Lean companion,
   first resolve the mathematical core and its split, then identify which exact
   obligations are already covered by the sources and which require a new proof.
   New production changes require the full publication and kernel gates in a
   separately authorized task.

The manifest is authoritative for which exact payload digests received a
comparison report and which review verdicts remain unresolved. Author-time
`unverified` fields are preserved as historical freeze statements; they never
transfer approval to a modified payload.
