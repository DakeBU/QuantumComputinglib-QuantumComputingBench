## Mathematical composition versus navigation

G1's arithmetic residual and range branches reconverge only after precision
establishes nonzero scalar margins. G2's mass branch and comparison branch have
different premises: target isometries and local closeness are additional inputs.
P1 requires the actual reversible supplier, a complete adder, the specified
Fourier sign and an available inverse together. P2's norm cap and Gram geometry
are separate ingredients; physical distinguishability needs an interference
input, not merely a global sign. E1 joins residual certificates, Gram meaning,
positive mass, raw error, exact preparation, positional circuit alignment and
the specified projector. E2 requires actual left/right preparations, the actual
left adjoint and coherent SELECT; its sandwich and coefficient error proofs are
alternative sufficient routes, not extra assumptions of one another.

Each full map records these inputs and scoped counterexamples. Library interfaces
make the definitions and source tracing compatible enough to compose; they do
not make arbitrary node combinations meaningful questions or automatically
provide process-reward labels.
