# Content-bound review protocol

The three author tasks own different mathematical uncertainties: finite-grid and
nearly-isometric Gram analysis; coherent phase suppliers and column-error geometry;
and residual-to-output / two-sided LCU compositions. The planning agent selected
these scopes before authoring. This scheduling is not mathematical approval.

Each pair has a distinct fresh-context AI reviewer. Phase 1 exposes only frozen
questions, assumptions, conventions, source locators and process guidance. The
reviewer may read the pinned local mathematical source, including its proofs,
and independently derives the requested result. Thus the phase is question-first
and source-aware, not source-blind. It is not model-response collection or a
benchmark-performance experiment.

The reviewer saves a derivation and exact file bindings before phase 2. Only
after the root observes that seal may the reviewer read the proposed complete
answer, rubric and reasoning map. The second report follows the repository's
content-bound reader-review fields: actual reviewer identity, core ID, whole
item digest, raw payload SHA-256, substantive verdict/reasoning, source checks
and remaining uncertainty. It additionally assesses rubric weights and valid
alternative arguments, reasoning inputs/joins, and overlap. The item digest is
the repository `qcb_core.digest` over the complete parsed payload, not only its
question or central formula. A separate raw hash binds literal file bytes.

Any source-locator or notation revision during phase 1 preserves the previous
question file, both digests, the actual timing and the reviewer's comparison.
Such an editorial rebind is not a second blind review or automatic approval
transfer. Mathematical changes require a new explicitly versioned question and
new relevant review. Failed reference/rubric versions and adverse findings remain
in history if revision is needed.

The root's final integration inspection is informed self/synthesis review: it
has seen author plans and review findings. It is never counted as an additional
independent reviewer. These AI task identities identify actual session actors;
they are not authenticated human identities or assurances of statistical
independence. No human expert approval, new Lean execution, source-paper-wide
seal, admission or split clearance follows from this review process.

Structural checks verify bytes, fields, source/graph compatibility and export
separation. They do not decide mathematical correctness, genuine independence,
novelty, difficulty or benchmark admission. Unresolved findings must remain in
the handoff instead of being converted into mechanical acceptance labels.
